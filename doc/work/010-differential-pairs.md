# 010 Differential pairs

Status: implemented on the engine side (2026-09-10). The value layer, the optimizer's pair path, `topology::simplify_line`, the pair placer in all three modes with via placement, and the facade's `start_routing_diff_pair` with its recorded event are all in. The LibrePCB side is on hold (user, 2026-09-10): LibrePCB has no pair concept and no naming convention is invented here.

## Goal

Milestone 10: route and drag differential pairs, as KiCad's `DIFF_PAIR_PLACER` and the pair dragger do.

## Acceptance

A pair routes in all three modes on a two layer board and replays from a recording. **Met on 2026-09-10**: `tests/diff_pair_placer.rs` routes the synthetic fixture in mark obstacles, walkaround and shove mode, places a pair of vias mid route and carries on below, and `eventlog::tests::a_diff_pair_session_replays_to_the_same_commit` records a pair session, writes it to text, reads it back and replays it to the same commit.

## What a host still has to answer

Nothing in the crate identifies a pair. `RuleResolver::dp_net_pair` is the whole contract, and a host that answers `None` gets `StartError::NotADiffPair` and nothing else changes. `doc/librepcb-integration.md` section 3.7 records what the LibrePCB side would need: that hook, three sizes on the toolbar, and a mode on the tool.
