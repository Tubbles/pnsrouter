## Decision: the parallel obstacle query ships off by default

Tags: #decision #performance #deviation

- Milestone 8 is implemented: `World::nearest_obstacle` cuts its candidate scan into blocks and runs them on `std::thread::scope`, which is KiCad's `pns_node.cpp:437` without a pool. The reduction stays sequential and ranks by (distance, uid), so the answer is independent of the thread count; `tests/parallelism.rs`, `tests/kicad_replay.rs` and two unit tests in `src/node.rs` pin that.
- `World::set_parallelism` defaults to **1**, which the work item asked to be `available_parallelism`. The measurement changed the answer. A `std::thread::scope` costs about 21 microseconds per block here, 42 for a two way split; scanning one obstacle candidate is about 2.2 microseconds; the queries big enough to be cut are 5% of them and carry a quarter of a scan that is 4% of a run. Eight interleaved runs on both boards show nothing better than the run to run spread and one thing reproducibly worse, the tail of mark obstacles mode, whose p95 rose 13 to 29% and whose worst move rose 25 to 32% across three separate batches.
- Root cause of the loss, and a deviation KiCad shares without paying for it: the candidate count does not predict the work. `hull_intersection` is hull segments times path segments, so a mode with short heads has cheap candidates, crosses the count threshold anyway and pays a thread for nothing. KiCad's dispatch to a warm pool is an order of magnitude cheaper than creating a thread, so it never notices.
- `MIN_CANDIDATES_PER_BLOCK` is 32 against KiCad's 8, for the same reason.
- `RuleResolver` keeps no `Send + Sync` bound. The parallel section never reaches the resolver (the clearance phase stays sequential, as KiCad's at `pns_node.cpp:346`), and a bound would stop a host from memoising behind a `RefCell` the way KiCad's own resolver does (`pns_kicad_iface.cpp:222`). If the boundary ever moves, the bound moves with it.

## Milestone 9: the dragger reference note

Tags: #kicad #reference #plan

- `doc/reference/kicad/06-dragger.md` covers `DRAG_ALGO`, `DRAGGER`, the `LINE` drag primitives, `MULTI_DRAGGER`, the optimizer's drag only passes, the host gestures, 28 errata and the order of work. Three TODO.md claims were wrong and are corrected: the dragger needs no `EDA_ANGLE` outside arcs, no `RotatePoint` at all, and `PointAlong` plus line versus line collisions only in multi drag.
- Corpus facts that set the order: six of the seven drag cases are mid segment drags and one a via drag; none is a corner, free angle, multi or arc drag; four are walkaround (three with `restrict_angles`, so the optimizer's `REQUIRE_OBTUSE_ANGLES` pass is required for three goldens) and three shove; no drag log holds a fix, so the golden is the uncommitted node delta and `Router::pending_update` is on the critical path.
- Multi drag and component drag were listed as non goals in PLAN.md from milestone 1; with the parity direction they are the tail of milestone 9.

## Milestone 9, slice 1: the dragger core

Tags: #dragger #decision

- `src/dragger.rs` holds `Dragger` with `start`, the mode decision from the clicked item and position (the three spellings of the `w/2` endpoint threshold are transcribed as they are), `drag` in mark obstacles mode, `fix_route`; walkaround, shove and via drag are stubs that fall back to mark obstacles until their slices. Dead KiCad state the note's errata name is not carried (`m_origViaConnections`, `GetLastDragSolution`, `SetDefaultShovePolicy`, the write only mode mask).
- `via_pushout_force` and the via move helpers moved from the placer to a new leaf module `src/via.rs`, above `node` and below every algorithm, so neither the placer nor the dragger depends on the shove for a routine that shoves nothing.
- `Line::drag_segment` (`pns_line.cpp:1230`) and its neighbour snapper: the snapper's tie goes to the first candidate (strict `<` at `:1216`), and `dragSegment45` declares a dead `s2` at `:1349`; both recorded in doc comments, neither "fixed". The note's pseudocode had the tie the other way.
- KiCad's `DRAGGER::Start` clears `MK_LOCKED` on the start item rather than refusing (`pns_dragger.cpp:331`); the lock check is the host's. Ported as is and pinned by a test.
- A latent out of range read in `dragCornerInternal`'s preferred direction block (`pns_line.cpp:768` to `:779`, `dirCount` counts only non empty postures while `paths[]` is not compacted) cannot arise in the port, which compacts both; recorded as a deviation.
