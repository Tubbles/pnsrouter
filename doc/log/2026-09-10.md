# 2026-09-10

## Direction: close the gap with KiCad

Tags: #decision #plan

- The user wants feature parity with KiCad's router. Milestones 8 to 11 are added: the parallel obstacle query, dragging, differential pairs, meanders, in that order; arcs after them because they touch the geometry core everywhere. Work items 008 to 011.
- Determinism with threads: the parallel part only computes, the reduction ranks by (distance, uid), so answers are independent of thread count. Expected gain is bounded by the obstacle query's share of a move, about a fifth in the profile.
- Differential pairs need a host side pair identification; LibrePCB has none, so a naming convention is part of milestone 10. Meanders are chamfered only until arcs exist, which is also all LibrePCB can store.

## Holds: arcs and the LibrePCB side of differential pairs

Tags: #decision #plan #upstream

- The user put arcs and the differential pair identification in LibrePCB on hold. The pair placer stays engine only with a synthetic fixture. Upstream context found on the forum and the tracker: curved traces were asked for in 2018 (discourse t/51), the maintainer rated them irrelevant "for probably 99.99% of all PCB designs" in 2024 and the 2026 request (LibrePCB#1710) carries "low prio" and "file format"; the file format has arcs in polygons but not in traces (t/323, LibrePCB#354). Differential pairs: LibrePCB#819 (open since 2020, opened by the user) and #1376 (closed as a duplicate in 2025, "not implemented yet"), length matching LibrePCB#820 (open, 2020), and the maintainer's 2019 view that "length tuning would be pretty complicated" (t/84). No LibrePCB pair concept or naming convention exists anywhere in those threads.
