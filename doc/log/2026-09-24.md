# 2026-09-24

## A pair route with intermediate fixes: turns after a fix, and no leg written twice

Tags: #bug #pairs #deviation

- Report (user, LibrePCB): a differential pair could only be routed a short section at a time. After each fix the placer would not follow the cursor much further, so the pair was built from many clicks, and the committed pair then held duplicated legs on both lanes (P 447500 to 4000000 **and** P 2000000 to 4000000 on the `TODO.md` board with fixes at (2, 0) and (4, 0) mm, the same on N). A pair tuning on it drew nothing.
- Cause 1, the move after a fix fails. After a fix, `m_prevPair` is the fixed leg's `EndingPrimitives()` (`pcbnew/router/pns_diff_pair_placer.cpp:856`), two segments, so the entry gateways of every later leg come from `buildDpContinuation` (`pcbnew/router/pns_diff_pair.cpp:599`): the identity gateway and four angled ones that step one anchor forward by `m_gap * SIN_22_5` and `m_gap * SIN_23_5` (`:613`, `:614`, `:640`, `:644`), `m_gap` being the pitch. The identity gateway fits only a pair that goes straight on first and turns later, which needs the cursor inside 45 degrees of straight ahead. The angled gateways are meant for the turn at once, but for the two lanes to stay a pitch apart through a 45 degree corner the leading lane has to be `pitch * tan(22.5)` ahead, not `pitch * sin(22.5)`: with the sine the two diagonal runs are `pitch * (1 + sin(22.5)) / sqrt(2)` apart, 0.978 of a pitch (0.989 with the 23.5 degree fudge), and `checkGap` (`:182`, called at `:251`) refuses anything more than 100 nm under the pitch. So for any real pitch none of the four ever fits, and a cursor outside the 45 degree cone of the fixed leg fits nothing. Measured on the reported board with a fix at (4, 0): every cursor with `|dy| >= dx` from the fixed end failed; with the tangent every cursor ahead of the fixed end fits.
- Cause 2, the failed move answers with the leg just fixed, and the fix writes it again. `routeHead` returns the sticky `m_currentTraceOk` on a failed fit (`:756`, note 07 erratum E12). `Start` resets it and the trace (`:645` to `:647`), but `FixRoute` (`:808` to `:877`) and `initPlacement` (`:656`) touch neither, so the first failed move of the new leg reports success with the fixed leg still in `m_currentTrace`, the preview freezes on it, and the next fix adds it to the node again. `NODE::Add( LINE& )` only skips a segment with the same two ends (`pcbnew/router/pns_node.cpp:718`, `findRedundantSegment` at `:1707`), and the `SimplifyLine` of the earlier fix (`:853`) has merged the leg into the segment before it, so the copy lands on top of that segment. A target fix does the same and finishes: `m_snapOnTarget` is set before the fit (`:694`), so the session ends on the stale leg and never reaches the target pads.
- Both are KiCad's own. The pair placer and the gateway builders here were verified line for line against `pns_diff_pair_placer.cpp` and `pns_diff_pair.cpp` for `FixRoute`, `initPlacement`, `routeHead`, `Move`, `EndingPrimitives`, `buildDpContinuation`, `BuildInitial` and `checkGap`; the crate reproduced them faithfully, so KiCad has the same two failures. The fixed leg in the node does not collide with the new leg's start (same nets), `m_currentStart` is not read by the routing, and `EndingPrimitives` places the new leg's anchors at the fixed leg's ends on the right sides; none of those contributed.
- Decision: fix both here as deviations, because the feature is unusable otherwise. `DpGateways::build_dp_continuation` (`src/diff_pair.rs`) steps by `pitch * tan(22.5)` and `pitch * tan(23.5)`, keeping KiCad's two rounds and priorities, and `DiffPairPlacer::fix_route` (`src/placer/diff_pair_placer.rs`) resets the trace and the sticky flag for the next leg exactly as `Start` does. E12 stays transcribed within a leg (the preview still freezes at the last routable position while the cursor moves); only its reach across a fix is cut.
- What stays: one leg is still a two segment fit per lane between two gateway sets, so a leg cannot go back on itself and cannot make three corners. On the straight fixture, a fix off the straight line followed by a snap onto the target pair needs three corners and is refused (no copper is written); the user places one more fix. The `TODO.md` sequence ending with a fix exactly on the 45 degree line through the fixed end, (9, 5) after (4, 0), fits a pair that ends heading north, from which the target pair to the east is out of reach. Both are the fit's shape, not this bug, and both now refuse instead of writing a copy.
- Tests: `tests/diff_pair_placer.rs` `a_pair_with_intermediate_fixes_commits_each_lane_once`, `a_straight_pair_with_one_intermediate_fix_commits_each_lane_once`, `the_move_after_a_fix_continues_from_the_fixed_leg` and `a_fix_after_a_move_that_cannot_fit_writes_nothing`, and `tests/dp_meander_placer.rs` `a_pair_routed_with_intermediate_fixes_tunes`. All five fail on the unfixed code. The pinned continuation gateway list in `tests/diff_pair.rs` now carries the tangent steps.
