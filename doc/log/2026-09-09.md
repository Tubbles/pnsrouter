# 2026-09-09

## The orphan net for routes started in free space

Tags: #decision #placer #rules #parity #kicad

- `RuleResolver` gains a required `orphaned_net() -> NetId`, the port of `ROUTER_IFACE::GetOrphanedNetHandle` (`pns_router.h:118`, `pns_kicad_iface.cpp:3020`), and `LinePlacer::start` places a route that starts on nothing on it (`pns_line_placer.cpp:1386`). It sits on the resolver because `AlgoContext { resolver, .. }` is what replaced `ROUTER::GetInstance()->GetInterface()`, and a second host trait for one method was not worth it.
- Contract: stable for the resolver's life, `net_code` of it zero or below, and distinct from every net a snapshot item carries. The third clause is the subtle one: KiCad's `OrphanedItem()` carries `NETINFO_LIST::UNCONNECTED`, which is 0 (`netinfo_list.cpp:315`), the same code as the board's net 0, but it is a separate `NETINFO_ITEM` and the collision ladder compares handles (`pns_item.cpp:188`), so a free space head keeps clearance from unconnected copper while being "same net" with its own fixed tail.
- `NetId` semantics are now stated precisely: `None` is a non conductive obstacle only (clearance always applies, never same net); netless copper carries a host id (KiCad hands its net 0 item through, `pns_kicad_iface.cpp:1691`, so two netless pads are same net there); free space routes carry the orphan.
- The three resolvers pick different ids: `FixedClearance` uses `NetId(0)` (its net numbering already starts at one), the KiCad harness and LibrePCB's `LibrePcbRules` use `NetId(u32::MAX)` with a `-1` special case in `net_code`, because their `net_code` would otherwise saturate it to `i32::MAX` and report a real net.
- Result: `simple-shove-1` replays at 28 added / 13 removed, KiCad's golden exactly, and is no longer ignored. Three of the four replayable goldens now match; `issue24132` waits on `.kicad_dru`.
- One existing test was passing on a broken route: `undoing_restores_the_direction_and_the_via_flag_of_the_stage` had its second leg collide with the first (null net) and asserted on an undefined direction. Its second leg now runs south, away from the north seed.
- LibrePCB gap recorded in `doc/librepcb-integration.md` section 1.6: the FFI maps net zero to `None`, so netless LibrePCB copper is a non conductive obstacle to the engine where KiCad's shares one net handle. Closing it needs the C++ side to intern a net for unassigned copper.
